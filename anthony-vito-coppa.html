Anthony Vito Coppa is an independent software architect and formal-grammar auditor. Coppalini Architecture is a reference-library architecture and research corpus that compiles existing certified formal results through machine-checkable verifications and closed algebraic proofs. The archive includes executable simulation instruments, runtime auditing software, and machine-checked Lean 4 theorem packages.

Working outside traditional institutional channels, Coppa built an exact formal grammar in which mathematical proof, physical field structure, observation, memory, and language are constrained to auditable, type-checked interfaces.

At the foundation of the architecture is a proved minimal two-generator basis for real 2×2 matrix algebra, built from Fibonacci transport Q, symplectic rotation Ω, and their commutator G = [Q, Ω]. From this minimal construction, the minimum 2-form operator Φ, Lorentzian signature, dihedral mirror reversal, and the complete 28-seam K₈ relation ledger follow by explicit construction.

On that kernel, he formalized Memory Return: a non-erasing calculus of time evolution that separates recurring visible phase from advancing occurrence history. It proves exactly when a reading can support a decision and when lost distinctions cannot be recovered.

The same contracts are carried into physical and computational realizations: Einstein–Maxwell field constructions coupling gravity and electromagnetism; the Suture Field Theorem; gauge and lattice verifications of the electroweak Higgs mass Hessian, relativistic spinor, and SU(3) QCD transport models; correlated Hylleraas helium and atomic spectral dynamics; a quartic resonant-obstruction proof for the collinear three-body problem; and Metric Mirror Symmetry, which gives logarithmic scaling interfaces between Riemann’s Xi function and Navier–Stokes concentration.

The work grew out of a field investigation of boundary discipline, one-to-one reference fixing, cross-domain operator transport, condition and constraint, and formal curiosity. The corpus developed from a relational self-help program into an 11-paper minimum ternary-logic field basis, Language Mechanics, and practical verification tools—including LLM Diaper, Mirror_Audit, Zero-Noise Runtime, and the Clear Onyx laboratory.

Its C++20, Python, and Lean 4 tools enforce static_assert, constant-expression reference preservation, negative controls, and mechanical-mathematical continuation criteria, including The Stopping Point of Enough and The Condition of Trust.

Each result is published with its premises, proof, failure witnesses, and a re-runnable receipt. Machine-verified closures remain distinct from documented open boundaries, and open horizons stay recorded as open. The current Verification page records 12 of 12 passing hash-gated cards, 48 of 48 exact checks, and 8 of 8 Suture verifiers, each hash-pinned.

The Recompute Kernel v0.2 is the smallest public entry point: one atom registry generates its executable results, Markdown ledger, and machine-readable manifest. It carries ten core exact-integer atoms, public negative controls, synchronized ledger checking, and the stated finite ranges in the record itself.
