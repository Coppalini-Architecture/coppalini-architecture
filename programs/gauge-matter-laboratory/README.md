# Clear Onyx — Gauge & Matter Laboratory v0.5.2

`clear-onyx-gauge-laboratory-v0.5.2.html` is a self-contained browser laboratory. A model enters the dynamic selector when it has a declared update law and at least one numerical receipt that can fail. The five retained atomic, molecular, and real-1D studies display `OPEN · check not admitted`.

## Electroweak Higgs sector

The Higgs carrier uses the Standard Model Higgs representation directly: a complex hypercharge-one weak doublet

\[
\Phi_x\in\mathbb C^2,\qquad
\mathcal L_H=(D_\mu\Phi)^\dagger(D^\mu\Phi)-\lambda\left(\Phi^\dagger\Phi-\frac{v^2}{2}\right)^2.
\]

Its centered finite-lattice update is

\[
\Phi^{n+1}_x=2\Phi^n_x-\Phi^{n-1}_x+\Delta t^2\left[D_iD_i\Phi^n_x-2\lambda\left((\Phi_x^n)^\dagger\Phi_x^n-\frac{v^2}{2}\right)\Phi^n_x\right],
\]

with \(SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\) link transport on a declared two-spatial-dimensional lattice. Brightness is the gauge-invariant radial quantity \(\left|\sqrt{2\Phi^\dagger\Phi}-v\right|\); hue is weak-isospin orientation in the displayed gauge; filaments use the gauge-covariant Higgs current.

| Receipt | Independent comparison | Required result |
|---|---|---|
| Local gauge covariance | evolve a branch and its locally transformed \(SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\) companion | transformed-doublet residual below threshold |
| Invariant modulus | compare \(\Phi^\dagger\Phi\) after the same local action | density residual below threshold |
| Radial mode | measure a small-amplitude radial plane mode | measured lattice frequency matches \(m_h=\sqrt{2\lambda}\,v\) and its lattice dispersion |
| Gauge-boson masses | construct the \(4\times4\) mass matrix directly from \(D_\mu\Phi\) at the vacuum, then diagonalize it | \(m_W=gv/2\), \(m_Z=v\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}/2\), \(m_\gamma=0\) |

The earlier real 1D \(\phi^4\) domain field remains present under its own name and remains open. It carries a real-scalar double-well update; the admitted Higgs entry carries the complex electroweak doublet and its gauge action.

## Reader conventions

- Brightness reads the selected local magnitude.
- Hue reads a declared phase or orientation coordinate.
- The SU(3) view uses RGB as a basis-population reader convention.
- The compass names its three coordinates beneath the vector.
- Dirac and Noether–Klein circular centroids return `UNRESOLVED` when their normalized first circular moment vanishes.
- The electroweak Higgs carrier is a finite \(2+1\)D classical lattice realization with fixed declared background links. Its mass receipt is the tree-level electroweak vacuum relation generated by the declared covariant derivative.
