INSPECT THE MODEL CLASSES, EQUATIONS, AND BOUNDARY

U(1) flux ring

Uℓ = eⁱᴬℓ; ∏loop Uℓ = eⁱΦ

Gauge-covariant, unitary transport on a closed finite graph. The receipts test local covariance, 2π large-gauge covariance after its required seed twist, and a nonzero half-flux response. The displayed holonomy is a declared reader coordinate.

SU(3) colour links

ψₓ → Gₓψₓ; U₍ₓ,μ₎ → GₓU₍ₓ,μ₎G†₍ₓ+μ₎

Finite non-Abelian link transport. General non-diagonal SU(3) covariance, plaquette and 2×1 Wilson-loop traces, and an evolved colour-singlet determinant are finite checks. RGB maps basis populations for the reader.

Massive Dirac

H(k) = kₓσₓ + kᵧσᵧ + mσ_z

Exact spectral evolution of a 2+1D packet. The receipt checks both branches, charge conjugation, norm, and the 2E mixed-branch beat.

Complex φ⁴ vortex pair

∂t²φ − c²∇²φ + λ(|φ|²−v²)φ = 0

A visible vortex–antivortex seed makes phase winding readable from the first frame. The receipt measures its initial winding and a time-local plaquette count, U(1) action, charge, and both lattice frequencies. The pair is allowed to annihilate.

Electroweak Higgs doublet

ℒH = (DμΦ)†DμΦ − λ(Φ†Φ−v²/2)²

The field is a complex hypercharge-one SU(2)L doublet on a finite 2+1D lattice. Its receipts test local SU(2)L × U(1)Y covariance, invariant modulus, radial mass, and the tree-level W/Z/photon mass spectrum generated by DμΦ.

Two-flavour oscillation

P(α→β) = sin²(2θ) sin²(Δm²L/4E)

The two-lobe display is a state-reader projection. The closed form belongs only to the unpulsed pure-α preparation; a pulsed state reports measured Pβ.
