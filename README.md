# Coppalini Architecture

**Canonical papers, formal dependency maps, and exact integrity records by Anthony Vito Coppa.**

This repository is the public paper shelf and reference map for the Coppalini Architecture. Its contents occupy three declared offices:

1. **Papers** carry fixed mathematical and formal constructions.
2. **Maps and registers** record dependency, source ownership, and open construction.
3. **Integrity records** preserve citation, path, edition, alias, and checksum identity.

No office silently substitutes for another.

## Formal maps and open constructions

Nineteen source-consolidation and field-ownership maps are retained as typed dependency records. One ultraviolet fixed-point construction remains explicitly open. They are indexed separately in [CATALOG.md](CATALOG.md); their presence does not promote them to paper status.

## Integrity

- [CITATION.md](CITATION.md) gives human-readable references.
- [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) carries repository metadata.
- [ALIASES.json](ALIASES.json) declares the two retained path aliases.
- [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS) covers every tracked file except the ledger itself.
- `python scripts/verify_archive.py` checks hashes, coverage, and alias identity.

## Scope and rights

The repository preserves papers, formal maps, and their exact public identities. Computational instruments and executable kernels retain their own repositories and are linked rather than silently copied into this archive.
