## II. Exact surviving method spine

### A. Condition

A condition fixes the office in which a candidate can be addressed and re-posed.

\[
D=(\Sigma,R,\rho),
\]

with candidate class \(\Sigma\), allowed re-posing relation \(R\), and promised read resolution \(\rho\).

**Final burden:** jurisdiction/versioning floor.  
**Do not merge with:** Constraint, content, truth, ontology, dynamics.

### B. Constraint

A constraint states what must continue to hold for continuation to remain admissible.

**Final burden:** continuation boundary.  
**Do not merge with:** Condition. Condition says where; Constraint says what must survive there.

### C. Admissibility / Gate

\[
\operatorname{Gate}(\sigma)=
\begin{cases}
\mathsf{can}, & \sigma\text{ admits lawful continuation under declared typing},\\
\varnothing, & \text{continuation is refused in the present jurisdiction}.
\end{cases}
\]

**Namespace lock:** \(\varnothing\neq0\). Refusal is not scalar zero, a missing physical object, an empty universe, or a false claim.

The January paper used metalevel \(\bot\) for continuation failure. The final keystone should use one namespace consistently and preserve the earlier symbol only as provenance.

### D. Non-vacuity

A declared office cannot earn closure by choosing a reader so coarse that every promised distinction disappears.

\[
\exists \sigma,\tau\in\Sigma:
\quad
\mathsf{can}_D(\sigma),\,
\mathsf{can}_D(\tau),\,
R(\sigma,\tau),\,
\sigma\not\sim_\rho\tau,
\]

with both addresses remaining legible.

**Final burden:** resolution floor / anti-vacuity rule.

### E. Erasure obstruction

Let \(W:H\to\Sigma_W\) be a chart/writer and \(\pi_\rho:H\to H/{\sim_\rho}\) the promised history quotient. If

\[
h_1\not\sim_\rho h_2
\quad\text{but}\quad
W(h_1)=W(h_2),
\]

then no reader \(C:\Sigma_W\to H/{\sim_\rho}\) can satisfy

\[
C\circ W=\pi_\rho
\]

on both histories.

**Final burden:** no downstream read can reconstruct a promised distinction erased upstream.

### F. Form

Form is the organized/readable state of the carrier under a declared description.

\[
F:X\to\mathcal F.
\]

**Final burden:** presented carrier/state.  
**Do not merge with:** Fit.

### G. Read

The method papers repeatedly place Read as the operation by which a declared distinction becomes available:

- *Formal Method*: read what each formal object permits;
- *Form & Fit*: READ is an explicit stage between MAKE and COMPARE;
- Canonical Method: \(\rho:X\to Y\) is a declared read-only office;
- Minimum Language Mechanics: every addressed distinction includes a reader at declared resolution;
- implementation lock: **never let a read overwrite the state it reads.**

**Current lock:** READ is the primitive verb. Reader is a declared realization/interface of Read at a resolution. The existing reader formulas remain valid; no Reader map acquires a state-writing inverse merely because a read succeeds.

### H. Fit

Fit is relational, not a unary property of Form.

Craft form:

\[
\operatorname{Fit}_{\mathcal C}(x,z;u)
\iff
I_\eta(x,z,u)\in K.
\]

Cross-construction form:

\[
\phi\circ T=T'\circ\phi,
\]

with readable fit

\[
\rho'\circ\phi=\bar\phi\circ\rho,
\]

and receipt preservation, when promised,

\[
\phi_H(R\frown o)
=
\phi_H(R)\frown\phi_O(o).
\]

**Final burden:** typed adapter / commuting preservation test.

### I. Function / Operation

Function is the operation lawfully carried through an admitted Fit.

The endomap kernel is a restricted calculable realization, not the entire typed graph of possible transports.

### J. Datum / Reference / Resolution

A comparison requires a retained datum/reference and a promised resolution.

\[
x\sim_\rho y
\iff
\rho(x)=\rho(y).
\]

**Lock:** reader-relative identity/return is weaker than complete-state equality.

### K. Return and occurrence

\[
\rho(T^k x)=\rho(x)
\]

does not imply

\[
T^k x=x.
\]

Occurrence appends:

\[
R_{n+1}=R_n\frown o_{n+1}.
\]

Hence reader return and retained occurrence may coexist.

### L. Receipt / Memory / sufficient history

\[
\sigma_\rho:H\to\Sigma_\rho,
\qquad
\operatorname{Dec}_\rho\circ\sigma_\rho
=
\pi_\rho^H.
\]

**Lock:** Receipt alone is not Memory; Memory requires recoverability of the promised distinction.

### M. Certified reuse

\[

\]

The reusable warrant retains source/version, calibration, uncertainty, status, and falsifier.

The minimum branch is

\[

(J,X,H,T,q_\rho,\sigma_\rho,
\operatorname{Dec}_\rho,C_\rho,W_\rho).

Dependency:

\[

Re-entry does not reset provenance.

## III. One final claim audit

Use one consolidated audit packet:

\[
\mathcal A=(D,C,Q,T,\rho,I,W,F),
\]

with domain, condition, constraint, transport, reader, invariant, witness, and falsifier.

The final audit order is:

1. **TYPE** — objects, operations, equalities, domains/codomains declared?
2. **SCOPE / CONDITION** — is the candidate in the declared office?
3. **CONSTRAINT** — does continuation preserve what it promises?
4. **TRANSPORT** — is the operation defined?
5. **PRESERVATION** — does the required invariant/relation survive?
6. **RETURN / READOUT** — does the declared reader close exactly where claimed?
7. **RECEIPT / LOSS** — are promised distinctions recoverable and losses declared?
8. **WITNESS / REPRODUCTION** — can the verdict be independently recomputed?
9. **FALSIFIER** — what exact observation/calculation would fail the claim?
10. **STATUS / RE-ENTRY** — what may lawfully re-enter as premise?

A later success cannot repair an earlier type failure.

## IV. Status and loss: keep once

### Status

Use one final register:

- DEFINED / SPECIFIED
- DERIVED
- IMPORTED
- OBSERVED
- CONDITIONAL
- OPEN
- REFUSED

Where useful, CLOSED — EXACT, CLOSED — TYPED, and CLOSED — OBSERVED-CONDITIONAL are resolution-indexed closure labels, not universal authority.

No node inherits the status of a neighbor.

### Loss

Keep these distinctions:

- **not exported** — retained in source, absent from current interface;
- **hidden** — retained but not exposed by current reader;
- **lost** — not recoverable from object or receipt under audit;
- **unpromised** — outside the preservation contract.

Loss is promise-relative.
