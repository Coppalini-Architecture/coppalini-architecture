\begin{theorem}[Noether--Klein scalar-symmetry theorem]

$Q^2=Q+I$ yields $L_{2\log\Phi}=Q^2$.  Equation (6) is then immediate from
the definition of $\mathcal T$ and $(Q^2)^{\mathsf T}GQ^2=G$.
