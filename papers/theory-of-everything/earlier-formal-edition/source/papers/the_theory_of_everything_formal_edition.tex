\noindent\textbf{Formal declaration.}
This edition typesets the controlling manuscripts and current formal receipts as
one source-indexed record.  It closes the exact $2\times2$ root, scalar
centralizer, phase mirror, Riemann quotient-and-lift reader, four-phase
occurrence form, $K_8$ cochain criterion, and implemented check suite.  It
places Lorentzian, Einstein--Maxwell, gauge, thermodynamic, biological, and
quantum-gravity content in the native equations and conditions identified by
their source owners.  Every relation carries its carrier, status, credit, and
completion condition.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Reference and credit discipline.}
A source carries formal authority in this edition only when it is a
publisher/DOI record, an official institutional data or standards record, or
a content-addressed primary project artifact fixed by a commit or SHA-256
receipt.  Historical locations without a matching byte certificate are kept
in the provenance registry so that the work is not erased; they do not carry
a theorem, empirical, or completion assertion.  The appendices retain the
full source-to-use map, named authors and institutions, archive state, hash
state, and the exact office each source carries.
