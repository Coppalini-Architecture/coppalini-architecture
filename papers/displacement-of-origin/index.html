Readable reality

A readable relation can still answer: Where is the reference? What changed? What stayed invariant? What authorizes the relation? What receipt preserves the occurrence? What would require correction? What would require stopping? Can another truthful continuation still occur?

If those answers cannot be recovered, calculation may continue, but orientation has been lost.

The displacement of the origin is the minimum failure: a system can remain locally correct while becoming globally wrong because it forgot what its calculations are relative to.

The repair is equally minimum: return to the reference; read the Difference; preserve the receipt; correct only what changed; continue only while another truthful return remains possible.
