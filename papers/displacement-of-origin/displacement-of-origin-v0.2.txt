READABLE REALITY

Readable reality is the condition in which a relation can still answer:

Where is the reference?
What changed?
What stayed invariant?
Who or what authorizes the relation?
What receipt preserves the occurrence?
What would require correction?
What would require stopping?
Can another truthful continuation still occur?

If those answers cannot be recovered, calculation may continue, but orientation has been lost.

The displacement of the origin is therefore minimal:

A system can be locally correct and globally wrong when it forgets what its calculations are relative to.

The repair is equally minimal:

Return to the reference.
Read the Difference.
Preserve the receipt.
Correct only what changed.
Continue only while another truthful return remains possible.
