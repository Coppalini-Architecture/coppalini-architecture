Metric Minimum

Bottom line. One rank-four coframe ΠAμ exactly types the Lorentzian metric g = h(Π), which carries gravity and its light cone, and the electromagnetic field Fem = χFsol, calibrated from the oriented solder flux Fsol = f(Π). On the Maxwell regular branch, Dirac reduction of that one constrained system is locally equivalent to the Einstein–Maxwell action. In canonical variables (γ, π; A, E), gravity and electromagnetism share one constraint algebra given seven named SmoothCompletion premises.

A coframe is a field of coordinate frames. Here ΠAμ maps each local spacetime direction into an eight-dimensional internal fibre. Its symmetric contraction gives the metric; its antisymmetric contraction gives the solder flux; χ fixes the calibration from that flux to Fem.

What the exact matrix proof adds. The signed fibre block of that coframe is Groot := [Q, Ωroot], one 2×2 block inside η̂ = Groot ⊕ I2 ⊕ I2 ⊕ I2, with Ωroot = [[0,1], [−1,0]] and Jrot := −Ωroot. Local key. In the finite chart, Groot is the signed metric block and H its mirror; in canonical mechanics, GEM[α] is the Gauss generator and H[N] normal deformation. The pinned source writes the latter slot G[s]; it is distinct from Groot. Fsol, Fem, and Fesc are distinct from bracket functionals; the contact row later records a separate source use of Π.
