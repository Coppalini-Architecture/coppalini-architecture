Acceptance criterion

A card counts as verified when all three conditions hold:

  1. Every file in the card's manifest hashes to its recorded SHA-256. A changed or missing file reports HASH-FAIL and the card does not execute.
  2. The verifier exits with code 0.
  3. The verifier prints its required marker line, for example PASS: magnetic cap.

Each card also declares its arithmetic domain (exact, or float with a stated tolerance) and its boundary, meaning what it does not check. The kit prints the claim, domain, command, result, scope and SHA-256 for every card.

Exact cards use Python integers and Fraction, SymPy, and number fields Q(ζ₇), Q(ζ₇, i), Q(√5), Q(√2, √3), Q(√2, √5) and the Gaussian rationals. Cards with negative controls feed the verifier inputs that must be refused and fail if any is accepted: S-2 has five, L-1 three, W-0 four (N1–N4), W-1 six (N1–N6), and NDA-3 four (N1–N4).

Where exact arithmetic is not used

Three cards use floating point: S-6, S-7 and C-1. S-6 and S-7 declare a tolerance of 1e-12. C-1 retains the released NumPy allclose implementation. All three are hash-gated and run under the same acceptance criterion as the exact cards.
