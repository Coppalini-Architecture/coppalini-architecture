Development / separated from the locked record

Work that is readable but not yet admitted to the locked record.

This area preserves presentation-ready development work without promoting it. Where a source is admitted, an entry names the relation it reads, the locked source it depends on, its present check, and the remaining admission condition. A hold without an admitted public source remains a named boundary.

An implementation read here does not extend a locked theorem, source pin, physical interface, or measurement. These are routing classes, not additions to the canonical seven-status key.

Common-sense reading rule

A readable page is not automatically a locked result.
