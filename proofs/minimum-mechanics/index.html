Claim dependency

These improve the dependency accounting without silently proving an unrestricted smooth Hamiltonian realization.

Jurisdiction & F

What a completed read admits

The horizon F carries prospective, separately addressed candidates. An admitted result requires an owning constraint, a typed gate, survival, zero drop/add/strength residuals, a sufficient read and a retained receipt. A completed packet can halt before any successor; a later contact receives a new address.

Admitted(J,x) → Typed(J,x) ∧ Constraint(J,x)

The 010u certificate proves that defined admissions preserve these conditions and that a coarse reader cannot reconstruct a distinction erased by its projection. It does not equate a formal limit with a predicted physical event.
