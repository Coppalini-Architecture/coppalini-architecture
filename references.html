SOURCE RELATION

A reference is a living address in the carriage of inquiry.

This ledger is exhaustive for the dependency declared in the linked paper. Each entry supplies either a direct source for a stated formal or empirical relation, or a named historical coordinate that the paper explicitly credits. The source keeps its native office; the paper names the relation through which it enters the present reader.

How to read this ledger

Direct source

Supports a definition, formal construction, measurement practice, or field adapter used in the paper.

Historical coordinate

Marks a named construction in the carried lineage and preserves its author, title, and publication address.

Bibliographic form follows the source language where practical. Translation details are named when an English edition is the access coordinate. Digital object identifiers and official publication pages are included when they provide a stable route back to the source.
