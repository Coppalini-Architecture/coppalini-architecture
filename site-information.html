Editions and research status

02

Each result and instrument is presented with its version, source and stated scope. Formal derivation, executable checking, numerical approximation and historical interpretation retain their own evidence records.

Research software is supplied as the identified release. Review its source, dependencies, tests and declared limits for the use you intend. Corrections enter the record as dated revisions.

Privacy

03

The site’s own code adds no account system, contact form, advertising or analytics scripts, or cookies. The HTML instruments compute in your browser; their controls do not upload model inputs. Explicit export controls save a file locally.

Netlify hosts this site and may process technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver and operate its services. See Netlify’s Privacy Statement for the provider’s processing practices.

Following a link to GitHub, a DOI registry or another external service takes you to that provider. Its own terms and privacy practices apply there.

Accessibility

04

The main pages use ordinary links, labeled controls, visible keyboard focus and text descriptions. Canvas-based instruments also provide source files and descriptions of their computations. Use the full-page instrument link when a larger workspace is helpful.

Accessibility corrections can be reported through the project’s issue tracker with the affected page and control.

Contact and corrections

05

For source questions, broken links, reproducibility reports or accessibility issues, use the project issue tracker. Include the page or release, the behavior observed and the correction proposed.
