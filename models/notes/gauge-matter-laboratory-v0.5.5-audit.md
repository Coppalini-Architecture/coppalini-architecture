# Gauge & Matter Laboratory v0.5.5 — public audit record

The v0.5.5 update is a reader and interface repair. It gives the open visual studies one selected mount and retains their scope; it adds no new scientific certification.

## Interface closure

- The primary canvas accepts only the admitted dynamic carriers.
- A `study:*` selection routes before the dynamic engine factory.
- One selected study is mounted at a time, with its own controls and numerical reader.
- The Study Reader rail states: `OPEN visual study · declared finite reader · no admitted main-carrier PASS`.
- An explicit control returns the reader to the previously selected live carrier.

## Study boundaries

| Study | What it retains |
|---|---|
| Λ Mirror · Δ′ | a completed-`ζ`/`Ξ` display reader; analytic reflection, planar mirror, and registry reflection stay distinct; Δ′ remains pending its own declaration |
| Real 1D Φ⁴ | signed field and boundary sector; reflected-sector comparison without quotient or field averaging |
| Hydrogen / helium / water | their own declared visual-study readers; no main-carrier PASS |

The Φ⁴ reader evaluates its declared finite energy, coordinate gradient, and tridiagonal Hessian for the stated mesh and inner product. Its numerical read is not a general smooth quotient, argmin, molecular-orbital, or material claim.
