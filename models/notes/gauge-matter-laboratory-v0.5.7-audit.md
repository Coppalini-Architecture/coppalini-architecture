# v0.5.7 Audit Ledger

## Signal-frame integration

Every selector-visible live carrier and every mounted study now supplies one declared signal object:

`FIELD / PLANE · COLOUR / COMPONENT · MEANING · MEMORY · MOTION · CHECK · SCOPE`

The outer frame renders this object before the field canvas or sandboxed study. It gives the common visual grammar a literal type without collapsing the models into one equation. `su3` is the only carrier whose `colourMode` is `rgb-basis`: its RGB channels are the three colour-basis populations. Every other carrier names its own phase, component, sign, quadrature, correlation, or registry-coordinate mapping.

The four mounted studies place their signal object in `#clear-onyx-signal`, retain a local `data-signal-frame` anchor, and are parsed into the registry during the build. The sandboxed study remains the owner of its controls and live receipts. The outer frame carries its declared contract only.

## Unified Study Reader

The primary canvas now accepts only admitted dynamic carriers. A `study:*` selector value is dispatched before `makeEngine`, mounts exactly one self-contained source document, and suppresses generic pulse, phase, rate, canvas, and receipt controls. The Study Reader rail states `OPEN visual study · declared finite reader · no admitted main-carrier PASS`. The change is a reader/interface repair; it adds no scientific certification.

\[
\mathcal L_H=(D_\mu\Phi)^\dagger(D^\mu\Phi)-\lambda\left(\Phi^\dagger\Phi-\frac{v^2}{2}\right)^2,
\qquad \Phi\mapsto e^{i\alpha/2}G\Phi,
\]

and a gauge link \(L_{x,\mu}\mapsto e^{i(\alpha_x-\alpha_{x+\hat\mu})/2}G_xL_{x,\mu}G^\dagger_{x+\hat\mu}\). The mass check first constructs the quadratic form of \(D_\mu\Phi\) at \(\Phi_0=(0,v/\sqrt2)^T\); only then does it diagonalize the resulting matrix and compare with \(m_W=gv/2\), \(m_Z=v\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}/2\), and \(m_\gamma=0\).

The test suite contains two failure witnesses: a corrupted gauge-mass coefficient and a non-unitary link. Both produce receipt failure.

### Water normal-mode receipt

The active Water engine advances the two independent normal coordinates by the
analytic harmonic map for

\[
M=I_2,\qquad K=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varphi^2),\qquad M\ddot q+Kq=0.
\]

A 100,000-step source check of the shipped update gave a maximum relative
mode-energy drift of \(5.90\times10^{-12}\), a sampled recurrence residual of
\(2.78\times10^{-17}\), a force-law residual of zero at the displayed state,
and a centre-of-mass residual of \(1.12\times10^{-16}\). A click applies a
declared velocity impulse and resets the energy reference; its immediate
post-impulse energy drift is zero. These are receipts for the finite
normal-coordinate display only.
