Notice:

We operate inside a readable condition called reality.

Further readability is not guaranteed. It must be protected and tuned so that a word points to what it names and a sentence stops where it has said enough.

Public sign

Read the conditions. Keep the direction. Leave the passage clear.

Public declaration / local language rule

Words must carry their declared reference.

In this notice, each term is used as a declared reference. Together, the words form a meaningful stopping point: enough to continue, remember direction, and leave silence for critical system flow.

When a sign has been noticed and its meaning is useful, it no longer needs to call attention to itself.

Orientation

Every frame needs a center.

Orientation differs between people moving through time. Equal justice holds those differences without losing the shared reference.

A frame admits a reference. A reference admits a center coordinate. The coordinate zero is held in the minimum chart: home, here, now.

Classical notice / source reading

A return is not its flattened map.

The exact relation is q(ξ + ϖ) = q(ξ) while ξ + ϖ ≠ ξ. The read returns; the occurrence does not disappear.

Source relation: The Displacement of the Origin. The five examples below are textual or hermeneutic companions, not premises, proofs, or physical evidence for the displayed relation.

Five companions, kept separate

They do not supply a shared theorem. Each preserves one ordinary invariant: a return is recognizable only when the difference, path, use, or horizon that carries it has not been erased.

  1. Gadamer — horizon. Truth and Method, 2nd rev. ed., trans. Joel Weinsheimer and Donald G. Marshall (Continuum, 1994), Part II, §1(B)(iv), pp. 300–307. The present horizon is historically formed, not supplied from nowhere. Reference.
  2. Homer — traversal. Odyssey 1.1–10 invokes nostos; the poem enters Ithaca without Odysseus (1.102–177), carries the prior sea passage in his retrospective Books 9–12, reaches Ithaca in Book 13, and turns recognition on the fixed bed-sign shared by Penelope and Odysseus (23.181–230), before closing there with the feud’s settlement (24.531–548). The endpoint cannot replace the traversal, recognition, or return. Text.
  3. Heraclitus — retained difference. DK 22B12, fragment transmitted by Arius Didymus in Eusebius, Praeparatio Evangelica XV.20: same river / other waters. Fragmentary textual companion to recurrence with carried change. Text.
  4. Laozi (Lao Tzu) — hub and return. Dàodéjīng, received Wang Bi text, ch. 11 (wheel-hub and use) and ch. 40: 反者道之動 (fǎn zhě dào zhī dòng). Textual companion to a usable center, return, and motion. Text.
  5. Jesus Christ — teaching and craft. Gospel according to Luke 2:41–52 places Jesus among the teachers and then returning with his parents to Nazareth. Mark 6:3 calls him τέκτων; Matthew 13:55, “the carpenter’s son.” The texts retain teaching, household craft, and later public action as distinct scenes. Craft is an ordinary analogy for repeatable fit, not a premise of the return/lift relation. Luke 9:62 is a separate saying about direction. Text.
