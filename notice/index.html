Formal type

Public record and detailed receipt have different jurisdictions.

The public record is designed for ordinary inspection: a reader can locate an edition, source file, declared condition, status, executable check, or correction route. It is the material this site is serving.

A detailed working receipt can preserve more than a public page should disclose. It remains held until an owning record names its inspection path, purpose, conditions, and scope. Retention protects provenance and privacy; it does not enlarge a public claim. This Notice neither invokes held material as evidence nor asks a reader to accept it by trust.

Independent technical examination

Return a specific finding to its source.

A reader may examine one record without adopting the whole program. The strongest return is an addressable one.

  • A reproduced theorem, source hash, compiler result, or finite check.
  • A counterexample, corrected scope statement, or established equivalent relation.
  • An independently replicated observation under its own declared method and conditions.
  • A missing dependency, unsupported transport, or proposed repair attached to the exact record where it belongs.

The Proof Atlas, Verification Register, Publication Records, Research Library, and External Formalization Reader provide the public routes. Technical returns may be filed through the GitHub issue register or sent through the research correspondence route.
