Coppalini Architecture is a public research library. Its entries connect a stated result to its source, proof or executable check, and declared boundary.

Field investigation

A practice of careful reference

Field investigation here means attending to how a statement is named, sourced, tested, bounded, and carried forward. The work combines formal methods, software, and public documentation so that a later reader can see both what was established and what remains open.
