Author and investigation

A field theory of readable difference.

Coppalini Architecture is the research program of Anthony Vito Coppa: an investigation into how physical and mathematical structure survives motion, how a reader notices change, and how a valid result returns with its source intact.

Its public standard is simple: every claimed relation has an owning carrier, a declared operation, a verification route and a boundary beyond which its proof has not been earned.

Research identity

A proof-carrying research framework.

The work begins with exact matrix geometry and advances through Lorentzian field forms, Einstein–Maxwell suture mechanics, variational and canonical Poisson algebra, constrained physical observation, and formal recognition. The mathematical result, physical interface, and measured evidence remain separately typed.

The public program now includes a 45-module Lean 4.33.0 source closure, source-indexed papers, reproducible verification packages, and interactive computational laboratories. This is a substantial formal research corpus; its certified statements are bounded by their premises, including the still-conditional global smooth-field Lean adapter.

Method / evidence

Make the chain inspectable.

Proofs are kept at the level they establish: a Lean kernel theorem, an independently runnable exact checker, a conditional smooth-functional composition, a calibrated model, or an open physical interface. The verification register pins the files and compilers; the research library carries the papers; the lab provides visual and computational readers.

Earlier source snapshots and experimental notes remain available in archival form. The archive is a chronology of discovery, correction and increasingly precise scope—not a collection of interchangeable finished physics claims.
