How the reader works.

Coppalini Architecture gives existing field results one common, inspectable reading path: named source → declared equation → governing condition → proof or computation → receipt. It is a closure of the named interfaces and results in this record, not a replacement for the native theories or measurements that supply them.

Language Mechanics supplies the grammar of reference, condition, constraint, reader, receipt, and lawful reuse. From there, the record proceeds through matrix geometry, Lorentzian field forms, Einstein–Maxwell suture mechanics, variational and canonical Poisson algebra, constrained observation, and formalized pattern recognition. Mathematical theorem, physical interface, and measured evidence keep separate offices.

The public record includes a 45-module Lean 4.33.0 source ledger, source-indexed papers, reproducible verification packages, and running computational laboratories. A common-sense reader can follow the declared route; an advanced physicist or mathematician can inspect the equations, premises, source files, and independent checks directly. Map to the literature keeps the public form, local construction, receipt, and stated scope together.

Mirror reader

Reality, imagination, and form keep distinct offices.

Symmetric Minimum makes a mirror read of the record: a reader can construct a possible form, compare it with a named relation, and keep the result only with its source and conditions attached. In this notation, e names motion and phase transport, g names metric, and G names the signed root form. Music remains a separate reader of interval, phase, and return—not a new physical force.

Imagination is therefore constructive rather than evidential: it can make a form visible, while a physical claim begins only with its declared equation, observable, calibration, and receipt. That distinction is what lets the same formal reader connect mathematics, physics, computation, and musical structure without confusing their domains.
