Four exact matrix tiles form the complete real 2×2 operator basis.

Construction

The tiles show I = [[1,0],[0,1]], Q = [[1,1],[1,0]], Ω = [[0,1],[−1,0]], and G = [[−2,1],[1,2]]. Flattening the four matrices as (a,b,c,d) produces a 4×4 coordinate matrix with determinant −10, proving independence. Colored tile accents identify basis elements only.

Scope

The real operator basis of the source. No occurrence ledger or physical interpretation is required for this algebraic result.

Source location

Theorem K.5: Complete algebra; Corollary K.6: Unique normal form
