Construction

Use the source’s inverse-square circular solution with m = μ = R = 1, hence ω = 1. The plotted positions are q(t) = (cos t, sin t), with tangent velocities v(t) = (−sin t, cos t), sampled at four quarter periods. Position scale is 112 px per unit and tangent-arrow scale is 34 px per unit velocity. Every snapshot has q₁v₂−q₂v₁ = 1.

Scope

The unforced central-force realization in the source. Conservation here is not a stability claim; the separate forced balance law keeps its own hypotheses.

Source location

Axial symmetry and a periodic central-force state, Propositions D.9–D.10
