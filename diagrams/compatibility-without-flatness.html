Construction

Use the source’s trivial SO(2) bundle, identity transitions, one global section, and A_a = aη + sin(u)dy, with u = t−x/c and c = 1. All gluing defects vanish. The displayed curvature component F_ty = cos(u) is sampled on −π ≤ u ≤ π, with three overlapping chart windows and one common affine drawing scale.

Scope

An explicit globally defined witness restricted to three charts. The chart borders are editorial windows; they are not physical boundaries or a spacetime diagram.
