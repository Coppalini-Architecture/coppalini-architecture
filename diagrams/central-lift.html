Central Lift, Representation Difference, and Retained Occurrence

A closed rotation in SO(3) lifts from I₂ to −I₂ in SU(2).

Draft 0.1

Read complete paper ↗Source reference ↗

Construction

The source path is diag(e^(−iπt), e^(iπt)), 0 ≤ t ≤ 1. The upper circle plots its first diagonal entry along a semicircle from 1 to −1. The lower circle plots the selected-axis SO(3) rotation as exp(i2πt), a full turn. Diagram connectors identify the projected endpoints. A small appended receipt marks the separate occurrence record.

Scope

A torus-coordinate view of the displayed spin double-cover path, not a drawing of the whole SU(2) group. The defining representation reads −I₂, so its difference from identity is −2I₂.

Source location

The central theorem; The defining spinor return, Proposition R.18
