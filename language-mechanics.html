Language Mechanics

A formal grammar for readable reference, pattern recognition, and lawful reuse through change.

Language Mechanics gives the ordinary act of pattern recognition an exact office: a reader compares a current occurrence with a retained reference, preserves any difference the comparison cannot erase, and records the conditions under which the result can be reused.

Scope 000 / Orientation

Read the seam before making a relation reusable.

LM 000 fixes the zero-coordinate of the field: a sign, a referent, a typed object, a reader, a condition, and a constraint do different jobs. No cross-domain claim is admitted merely because its words sound compatible.

At the seam, a reader must distinguish a returned form from an advancing occurrence. The result is not a private impression: it is a comparison with a declared reference, resolution, and retained receipt.

Determinacy inside constraint. When the input, rule, and jurisdiction are declared, the formal gauge gives the stated result for the stated inputs. It does not turn an undeclared analogy into a theorem or erase an unresolved difference.

Carried lineage

Five coordinates meet at the formal boundary.

Euclidean reference
Construction begins with named elements, stated relations, and inspectable steps. Geometry supplies a durable public grammar of reference.
Wittgenstein
The methodological seam: sign, use, rule-following, and refusal when a claim has not been given sense. This is a provenance coordinate, not an attribution of later formal machinery.
Gödel and Rosser
The formal horizon: a system must retain the boundary between what it has typed and proved, and what remains outside its present closure.
Craft tradition
Condition, fit, function, error, correction, and receipt are practical relations before they are abstractions. The craft office keeps an operation answerable to its material constraints.
Quantum grammar
Projective quantum mechanics supplies a native reader/transport example: a reader can return an equal projective result while the lifted occurrence remains distinct. It is an instantiation, not a derivation of quantum theory.
