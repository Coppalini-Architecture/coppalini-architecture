Scope 000 / Orientation

Read the seam before making a relation reusable.

LM 000 fixes the zero-coordinate of the field: a sign, a referent, a typed object, a reader, a condition, and a constraint do different jobs. Cross-domain reuse begins only with a declared bridge of condition, constraint, source, and resolution.

At the seam, a reader must distinguish a returned form from an advancing occurrence. The result carries a comparison with a declared reference, resolution, and retained receipt.

Determinacy inside constraint. When the input, rule, and jurisdiction are declared, the formal gauge gives the stated result for the stated inputs. Undeclared analogies and unresolved differences retain separate addresses outside that result.

Carried lineage

Five coordinates meet at the formal boundary.

Gödel and Rosser
